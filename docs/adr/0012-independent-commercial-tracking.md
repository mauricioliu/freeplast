# Independent commercial tracking, separate from the quotation and native request

The owner confirmed independent manual acceptance, payment and dispatch marks during the A prototype and authorized implementation for staging on 2026-09-11. They live in a separate, non-autoloaded `fpw_tracking_<request>` row with their own optimistic revision, update time and actor, rather than mutating Woo order/payment/fulfillment status or the frozen quotation. A correction therefore cannot rewrite the original request, invalidate a preview, recollect money, or imply that a real purchase was imported.

The form uses the existing owner capability, a request-scoped CSRF nonce and a plain first INSERT or compare-and-set of the previous row, following ADR-0005's concurrency precedent. Stale edits and unreadable records are refused. Sent is never editable here: it derives from the standing version's durable mail handoff, whose transport acceptance is explicitly not customer acceptance or proof of delivery. Filtering by the first pending milestone only organizes the inbox; no prerequisite is imposed on manual marks.

A separate row was chosen over adding fields to Draft Working State (which would conflate commercial tracking with pricing revisions) or Woo statuses (which would misrepresent a Quote Request as a purchase/payment). No historical backfill, partial-payment amounts, evidence attachments, append-only audit policy or multi-version tracking policy is inferred. The row retains the last actor/time, not a complete correction history. These future contracts require the owner's decision before extension.
